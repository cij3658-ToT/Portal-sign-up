Joining the Portal

A short guide to help you with the joining process.

There are two ways to join the Teams of Tomorrow® Portal — our online community, which is separate to the website.

Either you will have received an email invitation that includes an Invitation Link, or you will have requested to join via our website.

Either way, the process is identical.

1

Step one

Create your account

This is the first screen you will see. Once you’ve entered your name and a valid email address, you’ll be asked to create a temporary password.

2

Step two

Check your inbox

Once you’ve done this and submitted the form, you’ll see the following screen.

3

Step three

Enter your access code

You’ll also receive an email at your chosen address containing the access code. Enter that code on the screen above.

4

Step four

Change your password

You’ll then be asked to change your password. This is a security function.

5

Step five

Create your profile

Once you’ve done this, you will be able to add your personal details.

If the form doesn’t fit your screen

Use the <tab> key to scroll forward through the fields, or zoom your screen out to reveal more of the form.

Worth doing properly

Please do add a profile picture and some details about yourself, to personalise your presence in the Portal.
